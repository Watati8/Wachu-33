Chapter IV

Things I Hope You Know

This part is a little more serious.

Dear Wachu,

I wanted to make something for you that was a little more personal than a birthday message.

I'm genuinely grateful that our paths crossed. You've been a good friend to me, and I don't think I always say that enough.

Thank you for making time for me, inviting me out, listening when I needed someone to talk to, and being there during some really difficult periods of my life.

When things were hard with my mum, you listened, offered advice, and made time for me. You didn't have to take on any of that, but you showed up anyway. I don't think I've properly expressed how much I appreciated it.

And then there are the little things. The jackets, the Bazinga T-shirt, the Friends hoodie. I remember them because they were thoughtful, and because sometimes the smallest gestures tell you a lot about a person.

I appreciate your humour, your generosity, and the ease of being able to spend time together without needing to turn everything into a big production.

Life has been unpredictable in many ways, and I'm grateful to have a friendship that has remained a good part of it. Thank you for being someone I can laugh with, talk to, and count on.

I hope this new chapter brings you opportunities you're proud of, good health, meaningful experiences, books worth reading, films worth watching, and plenty of reasons to be happy.

And may Arsenal give you enough wins to keep you going. I won't be responsible for any football-related disappointment.

Happy birthday, Wachu. I'm glad you're my friend, and I hope you know how much I appreciate you.

With love and appreciation.
