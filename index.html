FILE 004 / PERSONAL CORRESPONDENCE

A note from
Watatingaroya.

Some things deserve to be said properly.

DEAR MACHU,

I'm glad we met.

Looking back, I'm grateful it happened.

You've been one of the most consistent people in my life. Through different seasons, changing circumstances and some genuinely difficult moments, you've made time for me.

You've listened when I needed to talk, offered advice when I needed perspective, and shown up in ways that I don't think I've always properly acknowledged.

I remember the little things, too. The jackets, the Bazinga T-shirt, the Friends hoodie. The lunches, the movies, the conversations that continue to a point where we have no idea where it started.

Those things might seem ordinary to you, but they've never felt ordinary to me. They remind me that thoughtfulness doesn't always need an announcement. Sometimes it's simply remembering what someone loves, noticing what they need, and making an effort.

When things were difficult with my mum, you were there to listen. I don't think I've ever fully expressed how much that meant to me.

I haven't forgotten it, and I don't take it for granted.

I hope this new year of your life brings you good things. I hope you find books that challenge you, films that stay with you, reasons to laugh until your stomach hurts, opportunities that excite you, and plenty of moments that remind you how much you are appreciated.

And may your Arsenal-related experiences be slightly less stressful this year. I can only wish you so much.

Thank you for being my friend, Wachu. I'm genuinely glad we met, and I'm grateful that seven years later, we still get to share pieces of our lives with each other.

Happy birthday.

From Watati Ngaruiya,
K24, Nairobi.
